LPC Art Pass (2026)

Most of the art players now see comes from the Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) on OpenGameArt.org, cut, composited and recoloured for LUMIRA ONLINE. Derivative works keep the original licences (CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / CC-BY 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0). Full original credit files: docs/credits/lpc/.

  • Monsters — "[LPC] Monsters" by Charles Sanchez (CharlesGabriel), bagzie and bluecarrot16 (CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0) link; "[LPC] Goblin" and "[LPC] Golem" — graphic artist Stephen "Redshrike" Challener, contributor William.Thompsonj goblin · golem; "[LPC] Imp" and "[LPC] Wolf Animation" by William.Thompsonj imp · wolf; "LPC beetle" by Stephen Challener (Redshrike), hosted by OpenGameArt.org link.
  • Buildings, props, decorations — "[LPC] City outside" and "[LPC] Forest tiles" by Reemax (Tuomo Untinen); "LPC Tile Atlas" / "Atlas2" compiled by adrix89 (see Attribution.txt); "[LPC] Walls", "[LPC] Thatched-roof Cottage" and "[LPC] Medieval Village Decorations" by bluecarrot16 et al.; "[LPC] Adobe Building Set" by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm) / William.Thompsonj; LPC submission by Daniel Eddeland (daneeklu).
  • Trees, plants, rocks, ground — "[LPC] Trees" and "[LPC] Rocks" by bluecarrot16 et al. (CREDITS-trees.txt / CREDITS-rocks.txt, trees · rocks); "[LPC] Plant Repack" and "[LPC] Terrain Repack" compiled by William.Thompsonj.
  • Base LPC assets by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Daniel Armstrong (HughSpectrum), Stephen Challener (Redshrike) and the LPC contributors — LPC base assets.

Character sprites: Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) via the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator — licensed CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0. Modified (composited and recolored) for LUMIRA ONLINE.

Interface art (HUD, combat wheel, skill/item/menu icons, minimap and target frames): LUMIRA UI sheets made for this project (public/assets/import/), cut into game assets by tools/build_ui_assets.py. Parts of those sheets that resemble other games are not used.

Earlier LUMIRA art set (characters, NPC outfits, equipment, headgear — public/assets/characters, chr_hd, npcs, equipment, kept as fallback): original pixel art made for LUMIRA ONLINE by the scripts in tools/art/. The world sprites in public/assets/world are now LPC art (see above). See docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md.

Fallback buildings, trees, rocks, props and monster models: KayKit by Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.com) — CC0, rendered to pixel art.
